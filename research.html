Research

My research runs along two tracks that keep meeting. One builds machine learning methods for problems where the data resist standard tools — sparse labels on a blockchain graph, free text in a permit filing, an agent population with no ground truth. The other uses those methods, and conventional econometrics, to ask who bears risk when a market or a policy shifts.

Working papers are listed with their current review status. Drafts are available on request; email me and I will send the most recent version.

Journal articles

Kang, H., & Lee, S.-G. (in press). Frozen Markets, Falling Prices: Illiquidity and Bounded Price Discovery in U.S. Neighborhood Housing Markets. Journal of Real Estate Finance and Economics. Forthcoming
Illiquidity forecasts lower future returns, the opposite of the financial illiquidity premium, and diffuses spatially across neighborhoods.
